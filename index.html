Welcome to the UMSI Career Development Resource Hub

Your Central Hub for Career Success at UMSI

Welcome to the UMSI Career Development Resource Hub! This is your go-to place for guidance, tools, and information to help you thrive as a student and prepare for your career. Explore resources for resumes, networking, internships, career planning, and more—all in one accessible space.

Why Use This Resource?

  • All-in-One Access: Quickly find resumes, career guides, networking advice, and internship info without searching endlessly.
  • Student-Focused Navigation: Organized by topic so you can find what matters most to your career stage.
  • Expert Guidance: Resources vetted and updated by the UMSI Career Development Office.
  • Accessible Anytime: Works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop devices for convenient access.
  • Modern Support: Integrates UMSI’s unique career programs and benchmarks against top peer institutions.

Gain Experience & Build Skills

Hands-on experience is key to career success. UMSI internships, credit-bearing courses, and project opportunities connect classroom learning to real-world settings.

  • Internships: Apply for roles that match your skills and career goals.
  • Project-Based Learning: Work on meaningful projects that demonstrate impact.
  • Mentorship: Learn from experienced supervisors and UMSI alumni.
  • Reflection: Credit-bearing courses like SI 390, SI 681, and SI 690 help you connect experience to your degree.
