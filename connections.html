Build Connections That Support Your Career Goals

Networking isn’t just about getting a job—it's about learning, building genuine relationships, and discovering opportunities that align with your interests. At UMSI, we encourage students to develop professional connections early and often to support both short- and long-term goals.

Why Networking Matters

  • Gain insider knowledge about industries and roles
  • Receive referrals or invitations to apply
  • Build confidence in talking about your skills and goals
  • Learn from the experiences of others in your field
